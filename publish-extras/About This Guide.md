# About This Guide

This is an **unofficial companion guide** for docents, students, instructors and visitors, prepared by volunteers at Montgomery County Community College (MCCC) for Mathemalchemy's 2026 Philadelphia-area visit. It is a study aid, not an official publication of the Mathemalchemy team.

## Credits & sources
- **[Mathemalchemy](https://mathemalchemy.org)** — the installation and all its underlying mathematics and artistry belong to the project's team of 24 mathematicians and artists, co-founded by Ingrid Daubechies and Dominique Ehrmann. The [Mathematical Connections](https://mathemalchemy.org/mathematical-connections/) pages are the primary source for scene content.
- **"Ars Mathemalchemica: From Math to Art and Back Again"** — Susan Goldstine, Elizabeth Paley, and Henry Segerman, *Notices of the American Mathematical Society*, Vol. 69, No. 7 (August 2022), pp. 1220–1228, [doi:10.1090/noti2515](https://doi.org/10.1090/noti2515). Written by three members of the team, this article is the source for much of the history, character lore, and scene detail in this guide — and it tours the installation as a dialogue between Harriet the bowerbird and Arnold the cat, well worth reading in its own right.
- **EntreEd Docent Guide** — the scene-by-scene activity format here is adapted from a docent guide produced by EntreEd, itself built on the official Mathemalchemy materials.

## How this guide was written
Text on this site is our own adaptation and paraphrase of the sources above, written for docent preparation and classroom outreach. Much of this guide was drafted with Claude AI and fact-checked by Scott Vaughen, a math faculty member at MCCC, and by others at MCCC. Any errors are ours, not the Mathemalchemy team's.

Verifying details in this guide is still in progress. If you spot an error, please let us know at cvaughen@mc3.edu.
