# The Mural (OctoPi's Mural)

**Math:** [[Fluid Dynamics and Vortices]] (Navier–Stokes) · [[Projective Geometry and Perspective]] (the Fano plane)
**Official page:** https://mathemalchemy.org/2022/01/11/math-connections-in-the-mural/

## What it is
A graffiti wall tagged by **OctoPi the octopus** — read it as a wall of equations, not a landscape. Green paint drips from OctoPi's bucket into spreading rings, and the equation sprayed above them in green and yellow is the **wave equation** those rings obey. In red and orange is the **Navier–Stokes equation** for turbulent fluid flow. On the far left, a triangular diagram of seven dots and seven lines is the **Fano plane**, the mnemonic for multiplying **octonions**. OctoPi's spraypaint bottle points at a **"ferocious wavelet."** Lower right is the tag itself: a **π** and the words *"Ceci n'est pas un poulpe."*

## Docent questions
- "The artist signed this wall *'Ceci n'est pas un poulpe'* — 'this is not an octopus.' Why would a painter insist the painting isn't the thing it shows?" (Magritte's *Treachery of Images*, and a joke twice over: the tag is a **π**, so an octopus named OctoPi signs itself with a symbol instead of a name.)
- "That triangle on the left has seven dots and seven lines — one line bent into a circle. It's the whole multiplication table for a number system called the *octonions*. So why does something named for **eight** need only seven?"
- "The green rings in the spilled paint, sound leaving my mouth, and light from that window all obey the one equation sprayed above the bucket. Can you find it on the wall?"

## Key vocabulary
Each term links to its Wikipedia article.

- **[Partial differential equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equation)** (PDE) — an equation relating how a quantity changes across space *and* time; the mural's two big equations are both PDEs.
- **[Wave equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_equation)** — the PDE governing anything that ripples: water, sound, light, a guitar string. Graffitied in green and yellow above the paint rings.
- **[Navier–Stokes equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier%E2%80%93Stokes_equations)** — the PDEs for viscous, turbulent fluid flow. In red and orange. Whether their solutions always stay well-behaved is an unsolved **[Millennium Prize Problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millennium_Prize_Problems)** — $1,000,000 unclaimed.
- **[Octonions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octonion)** — an 8-dimensional number system extending the reals, complexes, and quaternions; multiplication is neither commutative nor associative.
- **[Fano plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fano_plane)** — 7 points and 7 lines, 3 points on every line and 3 lines through every point. The smallest **[projective plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_plane)** (order 2), and the standard mnemonic for octonion multiplication. The triangular figure on the left of the wall.
- **[Wavelet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavelet)** — a short, localized wiggle used as a building block for decomposing signals; unlike a sine wave it is confined to a small stretch. Wavelets underlie modern image compression, and **[Ingrid Daubechies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingrid_Daubechies)**, on the Mathemalchemy team, built the family that made them practical.
- **[π](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi)** — a circle's circumference divided by its diameter; OctoPi's signature.
- **[The Treachery of Images](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Treachery_of_Images)** — Magritte's painting of a pipe captioned *"Ceci n'est pas une pipe,"* which OctoPi's tag parodies.
