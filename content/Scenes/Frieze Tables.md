# Frieze Tables

**Math:** [[Frieze Groups]] · [[Tessellations and Wallpaper Groups]] (2D cousin)
**Official page:** https://mathemalchemy.org/2024/05/14/frieze-tables-mathematical-connections/

## What it is
Tables whose border designs demonstrate **frieze patterns** — designs that repeat infinitely along a strip. In one color there are exactly **seven** ways to do it (the seven frieze groups). Let the motif swap between two colors and the count rises to **seventeen**, and it's those seventeen the tables show, worked in a **mouse motif**: the Symmetry Mice reproducing themselves in black and white down the border, so that a reflection or rotation can also flip the color.

## Conway's silly walks
**John H. Conway** — the mathematician behind the exhibit's Conway's Curios scene — named the seven one-color frieze groups after kinds of walk, and the official page draws each one as a track of footprints. The shorthand: **HOP** repeats the motif, **JUMP** and **SIDLE** mirror it, **SPIN** rotates it, **STEP** glide-reflects it.

| Walk | What the motif does |
|---|---|
| **Hop** | slides along unchanged |
| **Step** | glide reflection — ordinary left-right-left footprints |
| **Sidle** | mirrors across a vertical line |
| **Jump** | mirrors across a horizontal line |
| **Spinning hop** | rotates 180° |
| **Spinning sidle** | glide reflection plus vertical mirrors |
| **Spinning jump** | vertical and horizontal mirrors together |

Footprint diagrams and the two-color mouse patterns are on the official page linked above.

## Docent questions
- "Can you find a border pattern that only *slides* versus one that *flips*?"
- "What if I told you there are only 7 possible ways to design a border like this?"
- "Conway called these seven patterns *walks* — which one is your own walk down the hallway?" (Step: left, right, left is a glide reflection.)
- "Robots weaving spacesuit fabric or etching microchips follow these same 7 rules — why must they?"

## Key vocabulary
- **Frieze group** — classification of patterns repeating in exactly one direction; there are exactly 7.
- **Reflection** — flip across a line (mirror).
- **Translation** — slide along the line without rotating or flipping.
