# The Cavalcade

**Math:** [[Fractals and Self-Similarity]] · mathematical visualization
**Official page:** https://mathemalchemy.org/2022/01/19/mathematical-connections-cavalcade/

## What it is
Pages of a book "fall" and erupt into three-dimensional **Koch snowflakes** and fractal structures — a procession (cavalcade) of abstract equations becoming tangible 3D landscape.

## Docent questions
- "Here's an infinite length inside a tiny box — how?" (Koch snowflake: infinite perimeter, finite area)

## Key vocabulary
- **Koch snowflake** — a fractal curve with infinite perimeter enclosing finite area.
- **Recursive process** — a rule applied to its own output, over and over.
