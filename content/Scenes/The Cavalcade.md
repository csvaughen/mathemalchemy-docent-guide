# The Cavalcade

**Math:** A diverse collection of interesting, important and beautiful mathematics
**Official page:** https://mathemalchemy.org/2022/01/19/mathematical-connections-cavalcade/

Learn about the **fabrication process and the sources** for the Cavalcade here:
https://mathemalchemy.org/2022/04/23/cavalcade-fabrication/

## What it is
Pages of mathematics are cast into the air by the adult silhouette figure.  The pages whirl about, or flutter down into the [[The Garden and Reef]] or the [[The Great Doodle Page]].  See the official page (linked above) for a description of each page in the Cavalcade. 

One of the pages of the Cavalcade is **Arnold's cat map** which circles the feet of the adult silhouette, as a cat might do.  It's an illustration of **deterministic chaos** connecting back to [[Silhouettes and Vortices]] to [[Dynamical Systems]].  One fascinating idea with the cat map is, since it is created with a finite number of pixels, after enough iterations of the folding process that turns it into a chaotic jumble of pixels, if that continued, eventually, after repeated iterations, the cat image will come back.
