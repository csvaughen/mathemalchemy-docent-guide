# Fractals and Self-Similarity

**In the exhibit:** [[Mandelbrot Bakery]] (Mandelbrot set) · [[The Cavalcade]] (Koch snowflake) · [[The Garden and Reef]] (fractal growth)

**[Fractals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractal)** are shapes with detail at every scale, typically **self-similar** — parts resemble the whole. Built by **iteration/recursion**: apply a simple rule to its own output, repeatedly.

- **[Koch snowflake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koch_snowflake)** — infinite perimeter, finite area ([[The Cavalcade]]).
- **[Mandelbrot set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandelbrot_set)** — iterate z → z² + c; the boundary between "stays bounded" and "escapes" is infinitely intricate ([[Mandelbrot Bakery]]).
- **[Fractal growth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patterns_in_nature)** — ferns, coastlines, lungs, coral ([[The Garden and Reef]]).

Applications: image/video compression, antenna design, modeling coastlines and markets.

**One-liner for tours:** "Recursion is nature's copy-paste: a tiny rule, repeated, builds infinite complexity."
