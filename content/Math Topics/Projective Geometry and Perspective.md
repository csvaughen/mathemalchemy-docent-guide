# Projective Geometry and Perspective

**In the exhibit:** [[The Mural]] — the **Fano plane**, the smallest projective plane · [[The Lighthouse]] — **stereographic projection**

Born from Renaissance painters' perspective techniques (Brunelleschi, Alberti), formalized as the geometry of properties preserved under **projection**. Parallel lines meet at a **vanishing point**; distance and angle aren't preserved, but incidence and cross-ratio are.

Drop the requirement that a plane hold infinitely many points and you get **finite projective planes**, which keep the incidence rules and nothing else. The smallest is the **Fano plane**: 7 points, 7 lines, 3 points on every line, 3 lines through every point — on the Mural's wall, where it doubles as the multiplication table for the octonions.

Modern life runs on it: every 3D game engine, VR headset, and camera projection matrix is projective geometry at 60 frames per second.
