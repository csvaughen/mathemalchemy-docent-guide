# Activity: Scavenger Hunt

A single two-sided sheet for visitors to take around [Mathemalchemy](https://mathemalchemy.org/) on their own. The front has twelve riddles, each pointing at a creature or curiosity somewhere in the exhibit: solve the riddle, find the thing, and write down what you found. The back, "The mathematics behind the hunt," names the mathematics each object is about, and goes further than the riddles do.

**15–30 minutes. Any age, any group size, no docent needed.**

## How it runs

Hand the sheet out at the door with a pencil and let people go. The riddles point all over the room, so the hunt pulls people through the whole exhibit rather than just the nearest corner. The riddles are written so that finding each object never depends on knowing the mathematics behind it. A child can finish the hunt, and the back page has something for the adult reading over their shoulder.

The back page gives the answers away, which is why it's printed overleaf rather than beside the riddles, under the line *"Turn here after you have hunted."*

Everything on the hunt is found by looking. The sheet says so too: the installation is look-don't-touch.

## Materials

- **[[Mathemalchemy Scavenger Hunt.pdf|Scavenger hunt (PDF)]]**: letter size, printed double-sided, with the riddles on the front and the mathematics overleaf. One per person or group.
- Pencils.
